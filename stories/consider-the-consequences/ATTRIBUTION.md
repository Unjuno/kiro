# Source and attribution

This interactive adaptation is based on Consider the Consequences! (1930) by Doris Webster and Mary Alden Hopkins. Transcription source: English Wikisource and its contributors. The narration is translated at play time by the selected voice agent; it is not an official published translation.

Wikisource identifies the original 1930 work as public domain in the United States. This is not a worldwide rights clearance. Retain source contributors, revision links and applicable license notices.
